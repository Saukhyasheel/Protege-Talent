The Consultation

We don't start searching until we know exactly who you're looking for.

Most searches fail in the brief, not the search. Before we approach a single candidate, we sit down with you properly. This is not a ten-minute intake call. We build a precise picture of the role, your standard, and the person who will actually succeed in your team. That brief becomes the benchmark every candidate is measured against. It is the reason a shortlist of three is enough.

  1. i.

    The role behind the role

    What this hire actually needs to achieve in their first year: the outcome, not the job title.

  2. ii.

    Your standard

    The bar a person has to clear to belong on your team. We calibrate to it before we search, not after.

  3. iii.

    Fit, assessed honestly

    Who thrives in your environment and who quietly won't: character, motivation, and the way someone works.

  4. iv.

    Commercial context

    Your market, your sales motion, and the real pressure this person is being hired into.
